# Glossary — Simplified Chinese (zh-Hans)

Terms chosen for `zh-Hans.json`, used consistently. Address the user as 你. iOS system
terms follow Apple's zh-Hans wording (设置, 取消, 完成, 删除, 共享, 存储, 好, 文件, 照片).
Full-width punctuation inside sentences; one space between Chinese and Latin words or
Arabic numerals (`第 %lld 页`, `A = 440 Hz`), none when the argument is itself Chinese
(`%@曲库`, `来自%@的`).
